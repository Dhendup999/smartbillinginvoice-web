How Digital Software Fulfillment & Store-Bound License Activation Work

Smart Grocery & Retail POS is a 100% digital software application hosted at https://smartretailergrocery.netlify.app. Every store installation automatically generates a unique Store Activation ID (e.g., RTL-8F4A-92C1). Upon completing a Monthly or Annual subscription purchase, you receive a cryptographically signed license token (SB4-YEARLY-YYYYMMDD-NONCE-SIGNATURE) via email/WhatsApp. Simply paste your key into Step 3 of the in-app Subscription modal and tap Verify & Activate for instant unlocking.
